# Blank Canvas 🎨

Blank Canvas is a Web3 DApp that allows users to permanently save business ad text to the blockchain and dynamically generate customizable retro digital posters (Cyberpunk, Vintage, Minimalist).

## How to Use
1. Connect your MetaMask wallet to the BOT Chain.
2. Enter your Business Name, Ad Text, and select a visual theme.
3. Click "Save to Blockchain" and approve the transaction.
4. Copy your unique Ad ID, paste it into the View section, and click "Load" to generate your poster!
